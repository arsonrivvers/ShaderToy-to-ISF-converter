=== DESIGN CHANGE (operator, mid-execution) ===
The bank moves OUT of the rail panel and becomes a RESIZABLE, COLLAPSIBLE STRIP directly under the
monitor row. Operator: "Why can't we just have them live as a strip directly underneath the preview
panel? ... Resizable, we can adjust the amount of them on the fly if we want more shaders per set
(1x8, 2 rows of 8 etc), collapsable."
Rationale it is BETTER, not just different: it dissolves the Task 7 IMPORTANT-1 finding entirely —
recall's destination picker lives on the always-visible mixer strip, so an always-visible bank means
the destination is always visible too. No panel-open state to reason about mid-set. Full window width
gives legible names where a 280pt rail panel could not. And 8-per-row scales to the APC40's 8x5.
Controller rulings: (1) shrinking rows HIDES slots, never deletes them — grow restores; a quiet
"N hidden" marker prevents forgetting. (2) Show mode does NOT collapse the bank: configuration
collapses, performance never does, and firing slots is performance. Manual collapse still allowed.
(3) Capped at 5 rows x 8 = 40, matching the APC40 clip matrix one-to-one.
Salvage: Tasks 1-5 (Preset, SlotBank, SlotBankStore, ShaderUnit.sourceURL, the load seam) are
presentation-independent and UNAFFECTED. Task 6 (PanelID.bank) gets reverted, baselines re-recorded
back to a two-icon rail. Task 7's cell view and gestures are largely reusable; the container changes.
SlotBank gains a persisted `rows` and slotCount stops being a constant.
