# PER·SYS·TENCE

## Product identity and naming dossier

**Canonical product name:** PERSYSTENCE  
**Displayed wordmark:** PER·SYS·TENCE  
**Descriptor:** Visual Memory Apparatus  
**Bundle-safe form:** Persystence  
**Internal engine name:** REMNANCE

## 1. The identity

PER·SYS·TENCE is a fabricated visual machine: clinically engineered, responsive to deliberate
operation, and capable of behavior that feels psychological, residual, and slightly uncanny.

The name is pronounced like *persistence*. Its altered spelling exposes the system inside the
word. It names a medium-agnostic apparatus rather than a shader application, video mixer, or
single-purpose VJ utility. The product may grow into new renderers, analysis systems, control
protocols, generated media, spatial outputs, or forms not yet specified without outgrowing its
identity.

The product is controlled but not inert. It holds state, receives signals, transforms material,
retains traces, and produces a continuously changing output. Its intelligence is expressed through
responsive system behavior, not through claims of autonomy or artificial personhood.

## 2. Anatomy of the name

### PER

*Through*, *throughout*, and *by means of*. PER implies continuous operation and transformation
through the apparatus.

### SYS

The machine hidden inside the word. SYS makes the system visible without reducing the product to
software infrastructure. It carries control, state, routing, internal logic, and engineered
reliability.

### TENCE

A coined ending that evokes tension, retention, and persistence. It supplies the restrained
strangeness in the name: familiar when heard, constructed when seen.

### PERSISTENCE

The intact word remains audible beneath the construction. It connects:

- memory and the survival of experience;
- stored and restored system state;
- persistence of vision and the afterimage;
- feedback, accumulation, and temporal continuity;
- something that refuses to disappear completely.

The centered dots in PER·SYS·TENCE are semantic separators, not decoration. They reveal the
machine anatomy while preserving the spoken word.

## 3. Product forms and usage

Use each form for a distinct purpose.

| Context | Required form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark, title screen, launch artwork, major marketing display | `PER·SYS·TENCE` | `PER·SYS·TENCE` |
| Product name in prose, headings, release names, and public listings | `PERSYSTENCE` | `PERSYSTENCE 1.0` |
| Swift symbols, target names, filenames, paths, and other ASCII-safe identifiers | `Persystence` | `PersystenceApp` |
| Reverse-DNS identifiers | lowercase ASCII-safe name | `com.arsonrivvers.Persystence` |
| Possessive prose | canonical capitals | `PERSYSTENCE's output system` |

Do not casually alternate among `Persystence`, `PERSYSTENCE`, and `PER·SYS·TENCE`. The
variation is functional. The wordmark is an identity mark; the canonical capitals are the product
name; title case is an implementation form.

Do not write `PER SYS TENCE`, `PER-SYS-TENCE`, `PersysTence`, `PerSystence`, or `Persistance`.
Do not add a registered or trademark symbol unless an appropriate registration or trademark
decision has been made.

## 4. Descriptor

The canonical descriptor is:

> **Visual Memory Apparatus**

It should accompany the name when an audience does not yet know the product. It describes the
object without limiting its implementation or overstating unfinished capabilities.

The descriptor carries three specific meanings:

- **Visual:** the apparatus ultimately produces and manipulates visible material.
- **Memory:** state, persistence, recall, feedback, residue, and recombination are the conceptual
  center, whether implemented computationally, perceptually, or compositionally.
- **Apparatus:** this is a system an operator inhabits and controls, with the presence and internal
  logic of a fabricated machine.

`Visual Memory Apparatus` is a descriptor, not a promise that every memory capability already
exists. Product copy must distinguish the present instrument from future REMNANCE capabilities.

## 5. REMNANCE

REMNANCE is the internal engine vocabulary for temporal memory and residual behavior inside
PERSYSTENCE.

The name is a deliberately incomplete form of *remanence*: what remains after an inducing force
or stimulus has been removed. It also exposes `REM`, connecting the engine to dream state, rapid
eye movement, involuntary visual synthesis, and the unstable reconstruction of memory.

REMNANCE is not a second product brand. It is a named engine or subsystem within PERSYSTENCE.
The name should be reserved until a feature genuinely owns temporal or recalled state. A simple
cache, ordinary persistence store, or unrelated renderer must not be branded REMNANCE merely to
make it sound important.

### 5.1 Intended responsibility

As those capabilities are designed and built, REMNANCE may become responsible for:

- frame history and temporal feedback;
- visual persistence, decay, and afterimages;
- capture and recall of controllable states;
- recombination of stored or recent visual material;
- time-aware modulation derived from prior events;
- trace buffers and history-dependent transformations;
- transitions in which previous states remain materially present.

Its eventual boundary must remain coherent: REMNANCE owns what the apparatus remembers and how
that memory affects the present. It does not automatically own every preset, database, recording,
or file on disk.

### 5.2 Language

Preferred forms include:

- `REMNANCE engine`
- `REMNANCE history`
- `REMNANCE trace`
- `REMNANCE recall`
- `REMNANCE state`

Avoid generic sci-fi extensions such as `REMNANCE AI`, `neural core`, `dream processor`, or
`memory matrix` unless a future implementation makes the term technically exact.

## 6. Naming system for future concepts

Future names should sound like parts, states, and operations of one fabricated machine. They
should be concise enough to print on a physical control surface.

### 6.1 Concept families

Use these semantic families when naming new concepts:

| Family | Meaning | Suitable language |
|---|---|---|
| State | A condition the apparatus holds or enters | state, mode, phase, hold, recall |
| Signal | Material entering, moving through, or controlling the apparatus | source, signal, channel, route, bus |
| Field | A spatial or relational region of influence | field, surface, plane, zone |
| Trace | Evidence retained from an earlier moment | trace, residue, history, imprint, echo |
| Operation | A deliberate action performed by the operator or machine | capture, bind, route, fold, clear, commit |
| Apparatus | A bounded subsystem with a legible responsibility | engine, unit, deck, bank, stage, monitor |

These are a vocabulary, not a mandate to rename established technical terms. `Deck`, `FX chain`,
`monitor`, and `output` remain useful when they are the clearest names.

### 6.2 Construction rules

1. **Name behavior before implementation.** A concept should survive a change of renderer,
   framework, input source, or output protocol.
2. **Prefer physical and operational language.** Names should feel touchable and controllable.
3. **Use one meaningful mutation at most.** PERSYSTENCE and REMNANCE work because each altered
   spelling carries a discoverable idea. Multiple quirks become decorative noise.
4. **Remain pronounceable.** An operator must be able to say the name during rehearsal or
   troubleshooting without explaining its spelling first.
5. **Keep controls literal.** Brand language belongs at the product, subsystem, and major-mode
   level. Buttons and safety controls should say what they do: `BLACKOUT`, `CLEAR`, `CAPTURE`,
   `ROUTE`, `HOLD`.
6. **Earn evocative names with coherent ownership.** A feature receives a system name only when
   it has a stable boundary and a real conceptual role.
7. **Avoid generic futurism.** Do not default to neon, cyber, quantum, prism, flux, matrix, nexus,
   or neural language.
8. **Do not imply intelligence that is not built.** Physical intelligence means responsive,
   stateful behavior. Public language must remain exact about automation, analysis, and model use.

### 6.3 Voice

PERSYSTENCE language is:

- precise;
- restrained;
- tactile;
- technical without corporate gloss;
- psychologically suggestive without becoming mystical;
- comfortable treating feedback, residue, and instability as compositional material.

The system does not `unlock creativity`, `revolutionize visuals`, or offer `limitless
possibilities`. It routes, retains, transforms, recalls, decays, folds, and outputs material.

## 8. Application across future development

This dossier is the naming authority for new PERSYSTENCE product work.

From its approval onward:

1. New specs, plans, interface concepts, architecture proposals, and roadmap entries should use
   `PERSYSTENCE` as the product name.
2. New public-facing identity concepts should use `PER·SYS·TENCE` as the displayed wordmark and
   `Visual Memory Apparatus` as the descriptor.
3. New temporal-memory concepts should be evaluated against the REMNANCE boundary before using
   that name.
4. New subsystem names should follow the semantic families and construction rules in section 6.
5. Historical documents should not be rewritten solely to replace `ARShader`. Their original
   terminology preserves design and implementation history.
6. When historical work is referenced from a new document, write `PERSYSTENCE (then ARShader)`
   once when needed for clarity, then use the name appropriate to the time being discussed.
7. Existing code identifiers, target names, paths, test schemes, scripts, persisted preference
   keys, application-support directories, and bundle identifiers remain unchanged until a
   dedicated migration plan inventories and verifies every compatibility boundary.
8. A future rename implementation must preserve user state, code-signing identity, permissions,
   installed-app behavior, test-host configuration, build scripts, and historical test evidence.

This convention changes product language immediately. It does not silently claim that the code or
installed application has already been renamed.

## 9. Preliminary name-clearance status

PERSYSTENCE and REMNANCE emerged after a preliminary collision screen across web results,
software, creative technology, music, developer packages, and active product names. No obvious
exact-name collision was found during that screen.

This is not legal clearance. Before a public release, paid product launch, or trademark filing,
run a dedicated clearance pass covering at minimum:

- USPTO and relevant international trademark databases;
- Apple App Store and Mac App Store listings;
- GitHub, package registries, and software directories;
- music, games, audiovisual projects, and creative studios;
- domains and social handles;
- confusingly similar spellings and phonetic equivalents.

Search must include both the coined spellings and the spoken forms `persistence` and `remanence`.

## 10. Decision record

The name was developed from the actual product rather than from shader terminology. The decisive
brief was:

> A system you operate and an object with physical intelligence. Clinically engineered and
> precise, but also recovered, anomalous, and not entirely understood.

`PERSYSTENCE` was selected because it holds both sides of that brief in one word. `REMNANCE` was
retained as the internal memory-engine concept because it gives the more residual and dreamlike
half of the identity a real architectural home without creating a competing product brand.
